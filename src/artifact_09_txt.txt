===================================================================================
BREAKPOINT RELAYER OPERATIONS RUNBOOK
===================================================================================

1. Monitoring Relayer Sol Balance:
   - Check http://<HOST>:8080/healthz every 60 seconds.
   - If relayerSolBalance falls below 0.5 SOL, trigger PagerDuty threshold alert.

2. Gatekeeper Key Rotation:
   - Generate new keypair: solana-keygen new -o new_gatekeeper.json --no-bip39-passphrase
   - Call program.methods.setEventStatus(true, newGatekeeperPubkey)
   - Update GATEKEEPER_SECRET_KEY in Docker Compose environment and restart relayer.

3. Emergency Event Pause:
   - Invoke set_event_status(false, null) signed by Authority keypair.
   - All check-in desks instantly freeze without crashing physical client hardware.
